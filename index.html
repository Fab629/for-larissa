You Graduated!!!!

For my graduate

Hi Larissa. YOU GRADUATED!!!! Before carrots too, so you should be proud
You deserved a letter, even if I'm bad at writing them, so here it goes.

I’ve only met you about two months ago, so I unfortunately don't know what your high school life has been like before me, but based on how much you care about people, your dedication to school, and your commitment towards a successful future, I know these past few years haven’t been wasted. You’ve made an impact on people’s lives while simultaneously working towards a future self you can be proud of. I mean, you’ve had two months with me, and you’ve changed my life for the better. I really like that about you.

And while I wish a graduation we could've shared together is ideal and of course getting our diplomas together is cute, I think just sharing the accomplishment with you is all I could ask for. It would've been something out of a book to physically experience the moments that shaped the person I know today. The easy moments, the difficult ones, and even any embarrassing moments you probably wish I never saw.

But seeing the photos and videos of post-graduation made my stomach turn in a good way. Not because I was sad or because you got to graduate first; it's because I realized I don't need to be there physically to know I love you, and you're perfect for me.

It's because it hit me how real all of this is. Not just you graduating, but me meeting you, and us falling in love, and hopefully one day meeting and getting the chance to hold my bunny.

I hope now that you've graduated, you can look back and be proud, Larissa. I hope you remember all the people you called friends, laughed with, cried with, and shared memories with. The diplomas are cool and all, but I think remembering all those small moments is more important. I also hope I can be in those memories, because even if it's one-sided, I think every time I remember my graduation year, you'll be the first person that pops into my mind.

And I’m glad.

I don’t want you to leave my life. I’ve never explained how you’ve changed my day-to-day life, so let me tell you.

Every morning, I check my phone, hoping to see a text message from you, and when I don't have one, I always get mopey. As I'm getting ready, I check my phone at least 20 times to make sure you haven't responded yet. When I’m in class, I peek at my phone to make sure you aren't missing me. My last 5 minutes in school are spent thinking about how fast I want to get out of here to call you. When you sleep, I just wish you'd wake up and talk to me, but I know that's unfair and selfish. So I distract myself with work, but I still end up checking my phone 20 times within a minute. I end up looking at previous texts and photos. And every time you would wake up and send that message around 4 pm for me or 6 am for you, my body would feel a little warmer. I feel stress leave my body every single time.

So when I tell you, you're home or I missed you, that's what I mean. I think about you every hour of the day. And when I sleep and wake up in the middle of the night, I message you because it brings me comfort talking to you. Not to worry you, or to make you sad, but because I selfishly want to experience your love a little more. You have a whole life ahead of you, and all I want is to see you live it and experience it with a smile on your face. I’m glad I get to experience a Larissa no one else sees, and I hope that I get to be in your life through all the ups and downs. I hope I'll get to be someone you're proud of being with.

So, with that, don't stop putting all your effort into the things you love, Larissa. Make sure you keep studying, make goals, achieve them, and then tell me all about it. I love you, Larissa.

Mwah.

Yours Truly;
-Carrots
